DEVMATRIX
Dev Kit by FlightTrackerLED
The panel walks you through setup itself.
No app. No account.
  1. 1
    POWER. Any 5 V USB-C supply rated 2 A or more — a phone fast-charge brick works.
  2. 2
    JOIN. The panel says SETUP: JOIN DEVMATRIX-XXXX. Join that Wi-Fi network from your phone; the setup page opens by itself (if it doesn't: http://192.168.4.1).
  3. 3
    WI-FI. Pick your network on the live-scanning page and watch it join. 2.4 GHz networks only — that's the radio.
  4. 4
    OPEN. The panel shows its address — DMX-XXXX.LOCAL. Open it in any browser: that's the Console, served by the panel itself. It works with the internet down.
Full manual, apps & recovery: devmatrix.flighttrackerled.com/start
