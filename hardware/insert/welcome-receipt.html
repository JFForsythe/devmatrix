The panel shows SETUP: JOIN DEVMATRIX-XXXX. Join that Wi-Fi from your phone; the setup page opens itself. If it doesn't: http://192.168.4.1

GIVE IT YOUR WI-FI
Pick your network on the live-scanning page and watch it join. 2.4 GHz networks only — that's the radio.
04

The panel shows its address — DMX-XXXX.LOCAL. Open it in any browser. The panel serves the Console itself, so it works even when the internet doesn't.
