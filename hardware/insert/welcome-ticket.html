Any 5 V USB-C supply, 2 A or more — a phone fast-charger works. The panel wakes up and talks you through the rest.

JOIN ITS HOTSPOT
Panel reads SETUP: JOIN DEVMATRIX-XXXX. Join that Wi-Fi on your phone — setup opens itself (fallback: http://192.168.4.1).
3
GIVE IT YOUR WI-FI
Pick your network on the live-scan page. 2.4 GHz only — that's the radio.
4
OPEN THE CONSOLE
Panel shows its address — DMX-XXXX.LOCAL. Open it in any browser. Served by the panel itself; works with the internet down.
